# Upshift privacy policy — draft for the Mac App Store edition

Last updated: October 24, 2026. This draft describes the current sandbox prototype;
confirm the final build and add the publisher's contact details before publication.

Upshift processes videos locally on your Mac. It has no account system, advertising,
or analytics. The app does not upload videos or audio to a processing service.

## Files and local storage

You choose the videos the app can read and the destination of each export. Upshift
creates private temporary working copies while processing, then removes them when
a job finishes or is cancelled. A crash can leave temporary copies until they are
cleaned up by the operating system. Exported videos remain in the location you choose.

Upshift stores an app-scoped file bookmark locally to remember the last completed
export. Processing logs shown in the app may contain local filenames and technical
errors. These logs are not sent to the publisher. If you share a log with support,
review it for personal information first.

If you choose a location managed by iCloud Drive or another storage provider,
that provider's syncing and privacy practices apply independently of Upshift.

## Network access and third parties

The current sandbox prototype has no network entitlement. Its AI engine and model
are included with the app. There is no in-app model download or footage upload.
Apple's operating system and App Store may independently collect diagnostics or
purchase information under Apple's own privacy practices and your device settings.
